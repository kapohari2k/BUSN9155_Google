How to review your cleaning service area across Google and your website

If you have narrowed or shifted the Perth suburbs your cleaning business serves, you may be looking for how to change your Google Business Profile service area and whether your website pages need updating at the same time.

This guide walks through a five-step check you can complete yourself. Not every suburb adjustment requires edits across every page or a paid review, and using this checklist independently without enquiring is a completely valid outcome.

SELF-CHECK OVERVIEW

What this 5-step guide covers

  • 1. Operating list: Confirming your active Perth suburbs and travel limits first.
  • 2. Google Business Profile: Checking your listed service areas (up to 20 suburbs or postcodes).
  • 3. Website pages: Spotting conflicting "All Perth Metro" statements in headers, footers, and FAQs.
  • 4. Enquiry prompts: Asking for the customer's suburb up front on your quote form.

Includes a direct link to official Google Business Profile Help (Answer 9157481).

Why checking your Google Business Profile service area is only one part of the picture

When you update your Google Business Profile service area, Google updates the geographic areas listed on your profile, allowing up to 20 suburbs, cities, or postcodes. However, prospective customers who click through to your website still read your homepage introduction, footer suburb list, and quote form.

Checking all three touchpoints together takes about 15 minutes and helps you decide which places actually need editing and which can stay as they are.

Step 1 · Operating reality · Typical owner action

Before logging into any platform, list the specific Perth suburbs, postcodes, or local government areas you actively want to accept for your main cleaning services.

When an edit is needed: Only needed when your preferred travel radius or suburb focus has genuinely changed.

Step 2 · Google Business Profile · Publisher: Business owner

Sign in to your Google Business Profile, select "Edit profile", open the "Location" tab, and review the "Service area" section. Google allows up to 20 specific service areas by suburb, city, or postcode rather than a kilometre radius.

When an edit is needed: Update this list if it still shows whole-of-Perth coverage or outer suburbs you have stopped visiting.

Step 3 · Website Pages · Publisher: Owner or web contact

Read your homepage opening section and site-wide footer. Look for broad default phrases such as "All Perth Metro Areas" that may contradict your narrower Google Business Profile list.

When an edit is needed: Update if the website promises metro-wide travel that you now decline. If your website already names the right corridor (for example, "Inner Northern Perth Suburbs") and you only dropped one outlying suburb, a full page rewrite may not be necessary.

Step 4 · Website Pages · Publisher: Web contact (using owner-confirmed suburbs)

Check whether different services have different travel rules (for example, travelling further for a full vacate clean than for a two-hour fortnightly home clean) and whether your pages explain that distinction.

When an edit is needed: Clarify only if customers frequently enquire for small jobs outside your regular corridor.

Step 5 · Enquiry & Quote Flow · Publisher: Web contact or form administrator

Open your contact or quote request page as a customer would. Check whether the form explicitly asks for the property suburb alongside the clean type and property size.

When an edit is needed: Add a suburb field or a clear helper prompt above the message box if enquiries regularly arrive without a location.

ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE)

Example: Narrowing recurring home cleaning suburbs in northern Perth

Suppose a fictional owner-operated residential cleaning business based near Stirling decides to focus recurring fortnightly cleans within inner northern and coastal suburbs (such as Karrinyup, Scarborough, Innaloo, and Mount Hawthorn) rather than driving south of the river.

  • What the owner updates directly: Replaces "Perth WA" in the Google Business Profile Service Area settings with the 12 core northern suburbs they want to serve.
  • What the web contact updates: Replaces the old "All Perth Metro Suburbs" footer line with a clear statement of the northern suburb corridor, and adds "Tell us your suburb, clean type and property size." above the quote form.
  • What stays unchanged: The existing pricing guide and 48-hour rescheduling policy on the website still match how the business operates, so those sections are left untouched.
