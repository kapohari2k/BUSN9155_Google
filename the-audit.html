AT A GLANCE

How this review is structured

  • Owner input: You confirm your current services, prices, policies, and preferred suburbs first.
  • Deliverables: Prioritised findings, drafted replacement wording, placement instructions, and a review call.
  • Publishing: Completed by you or your web contact (separate web developer publishing fees may apply).
  • Commercial terms: Scope, review fee, and timing are agreed in writing before any paid work starts.

Excludes direct CMS logins, comprehensive SEO, website rebuilding, and ongoing retainers.

01. AGREED SCOPE

What we review

Every review covers a clearly defined set of public pages agreed with you before paid work begins:

  • Agreed website pages: Your homepage, core residential cleaning service pages, FAQ or booking policy notes, and footer coverage summaries.
  • Google Business Profile details: Your publicly listed business description, service categories, and listed Perth service area suburbs or postcodes.
  • Enquiry & quote-request content: Your contact page, quote request buttons, form field labels, and helper instructions shown to prospective customers.

02. DELIVERABLES

What you receive

Instead of a generic automated report, you receive a practical document prepared specifically for your business:

  • Prioritised findings with reasons: Clear explanation of where public wording conflicts with your current operations or leaves out key details.
  • Ready-to-use replacement wording: Drafted Australian-English copy ready for you to approve or adjust, plus explicit notes on items that are already clear and should remain unchanged.
  • Placement & publisher split: Exact page and section instructions showing whether each change is best published by you or your web contact.
  • Review & correction call: A structured call to walk through every finding and refine any wording so it is accurate before publishing.

03. EXCLUSIONS & TERMS

What is excluded

To keep scope transparent and avoid overlap with your web developer, the following are excluded:

  • Direct website implementation: You or your web contact publish the final changes. We do not log into your website CMS or Google account.
  • Comprehensive SEO or rebuilds: Technical SEO, backlink campaigns, keyword rank tracking, and website redesigns are not included.
  • Ongoing management: This is a one-off review with no recurring retainer.

Fees & publishing costs: Exact scope, review fee, and delivery timing are agreed in writing before paid work starts. Any fee charged by your web contact to publish edits on your website is separate.

ITEM 01 · Homepage & Services Page Quote Buttons
High priority update · Publisher: Web contact (after owner confirmation)

Quote link gives no guidance on what details to include

What we observed (fictional example)

Fictional example: The primary "Request a Quote" link opens a generic message box with only Name, Email, and Message fields, without telling the customer what property or booking details are needed.

Why this matters

When prospective customers do not know what information is needed to estimate a job, enquiries often arrive without a suburb, service type, or home size. That creates avoidable follow-up messages before you can even confirm whether the home is in your coverage area.

Ready-to-use replacement wording (subject to owner confirmation)

"Tell us your suburb, clean type and property size."

Placement instruction: Place directly above the enquiry message box and below the heading on the Request a Quote section (or as helper text on the main enquiry form).

Owner confirmation note: Subject to owner confirmation of the exact details required before quoting (for example, whether bathroom count or pet details should also be requested).

ITEM 02 · Google Business Profile vs Website Footer & Service Page
High priority update · Publisher: Owner (Google profile) & Web contact (website)

Listed service area on the website is broader than current preferred suburbs

What we observed (fictional example)

Fictional example: The website footer states "Servicing all Perth metro suburbs north and south of the river", while the owner now focuses on recurring residential cleans within 15 minutes of Stirling and inner northern suburbs.

Why this matters

Inconsistent coverage statements between your Google Business Profile and website pages lead to enquiries from outer suburbs you no longer serve, taking time to decline and creating confusion about where you actually travel.

Ready-to-use replacement wording (subject to owner confirmation)

"We provide regular residential cleaning across inner northern and coastal Perth suburbs, including Stirling, Karrinyup, Scarborough, Innaloo, Mount Hawthorn, and surrounding areas."

Placement instruction: Update the Google Business Profile Service Area list (up to 20 suburbs or postcodes) via the owner Google account, and replace the broad metro line in the website footer and Home introduction.

Owner confirmation note: Subject to owner confirmation of the final suburb list and whether nearby suburbs are accepted for fortnightly bookings.

ITEM 03 · Regular Home Cleaning Service Page
Medium priority update · Publisher: Web contact (after owner confirmation)

Standard inclusions vs extra tasks are not separated clearly

What we observed (fictional example)

Fictional example: The Regular Cleaning page mentions kitchens, bathrooms, floors, ovens, and interior windows in one general paragraph without distinguishing routine fortnightly tasks from add-on extras.

Why this matters

Grouping routine upkeep with periodic extras makes it harder for homeowners to understand what a standard visit covers and which tasks require extra time and a separate quote.

Ready-to-use replacement wording (subject to owner confirmation)

"Every regular clean covers kitchen benches and sinks, bathroom sanitising, dusting, and vacuuming and mopping hard floors. Inside oven cleaning, internal windows, and deep skirting board detailing can be added by prior arrangement."

Placement instruction: Replace the second paragraph under "What Our Regular Home Clean Covers" on the Services page with two short sub-sections: "Included in every visit" and "Available by prior request".

Owner confirmation note: Subject to owner confirmation of standard routine tasks and which add-on tasks are currently offered.

ITEM 04 · Booking Enquiry Page: Access & Rescheduling Note
Clear (keep unchanged) · Publisher: No edit required

Entry instructions and 48-hour rescheduling notice are already clear

What we observed (fictional example)

Fictional example: The existing page states: "You do not need to be home during your clean if safe key or lockbox access is arranged in advance. Please give at least 48 hours notice if you need to reschedule a regular booking."

Why this matters

This wording is plain, specific, and matches the owner-confirmed operating policy. Rewriting clear copy adds unnecessary editing cost and risks introducing ambiguity where none exists.

Recommendation (keep existing wording)

"No replacement wording needed. Keep current text unchanged."

Placement instruction: Retain in its current position under "How Bookings Work" on the Enquiry page.

Owner confirmation note: Confirmed accurate against current operating policy; flagged as "Keep unchanged" so neither the owner nor the web contact spends time editing it.

How the review works from initial enquiry to handoff

Every step is designed to keep your time commitment predictable and ensure you only pay once scope, fee, and timing are agreed.

STEP 01

Initial enquiry & suitability check

Send your website URL and what you want checked. We inspect your public pages and confirm whether a review is suitable or if a simpler DIY update makes more sense.

STEP 02

Written scope & owner confirmation

We agree on the exact pages, review fee, and turnaround time in writing. You then confirm your current services, inclusions, suburbs, and quote requirements.

STEP 03

Comparison & replacement wording

We review your nominated pages, Google Business Profile, and quote prompts against your confirmed details and prepare your prioritised findings and replacement copy.

STEP 04

Review call & publishing handoff

We hold a review call to talk through each recommendation, refine any wording together, and hand over the final pack ready for you or your web contact to publish.
