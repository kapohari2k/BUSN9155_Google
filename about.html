Method & Operating Principles · Perth Residential Cleaning Focus

A structured method for reviewing cleaning business websites and enquiry steps

Local Lift is a proposed consultancy service built around a single practical task: comparing what your website, Google Business Profile, and enquiry form currently tell prospective customers against the services, suburbs, and booking policies you actually run today.

Why credibility comes from a transparent process

When an owner-manager runs an established residential cleaning business, the website set up years ago often falls behind day-to-day changes. You might narrow your preferred Perth suburbs to reduce unpaid travel time, stop offering one-off oven cleans by themselves, or require specific property details before quoting.

Checking every page, footer note, Google Business Profile field, and form prompt takes focused desk time, and writing clear replacement wording takes further effort before your web contact can even publish an update. Our proposed value is completing that comparison and drafting work methodically.

PRINCIPLE 01

Owner-confirmed truth comes first

We never guess your prices, policies, or inclusions. You confirm your actual operating rules before we evaluate a single sentence on your website or Google profile.

PRINCIPLE 02

Specific wording, not vague critique

Instead of handing over an abstract scorecard or generic website content audit Perth checklist, every priority finding includes drafted replacement wording and exact placement instructions.

PRINCIPLE 03

Preserve what is already clear

If a service description or booking policy on your site is already clear and accurate, we mark it to remain unchanged so you do not pay a web contact to edit working pages.

PRINCIPLE 04

Realistic boundaries without hype

Clearer service and enquiry wording helps customers understand what you offer and how to request a quote, but we do not promise search rankings, enquiry volume, or guaranteed conversions.

How owner involvement and publishing handoff work

Many established cleaning business owners can update their own Google Business Profile, while relying on a web designer, IT contact, or family member to edit website pages. We structure the review so each person knows their exact part.

Stage Business owner role Local Lift role Web contact role (if used)
1. Scope & confirmation Confirm which pages to review and share current services, suburbs, and quote rules. Agree scope, fee, and timing in writing before any paid work starts. Not required at this stage (no passwords or site logins needed).
2. Comparison & drafting No desk work required while the review is prepared. Compare public pages, Google profile, and enquiry steps; draft prioritised findings and replacement copy. No involvement required during drafting.
3. Review & refinement call Walk through recommendations, ask questions, and confirm any wording adjustments. Explain reasons for each item and update any phrasing so it matches your business rules. Optional attendance if the owner prefers.
4. Publishing updates Approve final wording and update owner-controlled items (such as Google Business Profile service areas). Provide clean placement instructions separated by publisher role. Paste approved replacement wording into the nominated website sections (separate publishing fees may apply).

How Local Lift compares with other options

Our package is not the only way to update your website or Google profile, and other providers can produce similar outputs. Choosing the right path depends on how much you already know about the changes needed:

Doing it yourself (DIY)

Best when you have time to check your own pages and only need minor edits. Our free Coverage checklist is designed to support DIY checks.

Briefing your existing web provider directly

Best when you already know the exact wording you want replaced. Keep in mind that web developers typically publish wording you supply rather than cross-checking your operating rules across your Google profile and service pages.

General website copywriting or SEO audit services

A general copywriter or digital agency can rewrite full websites or run technical SEO campaigns, which suits businesses rebranding or building new pages from scratch. Local Lift focuses narrowly on comparing existing pages against your current cleaning operations so you only change what needs clarifying.
