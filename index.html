What a cleaning business website audit checks and delivers

Over time, an established residential cleaning business often adjusts how it operates: narrowing preferred Perth suburbs, refining what is included in a standard visit, or changing minimum booking rules. Our review checks whether your public information still matches how you work today.

01. SERVICES & BOUNDARIES

Are services, inclusions, and exclusions understandable?

We check whether prospective customers can tell the difference between your core domestic cleans, periodic deep or vacate cleans, and tasks that are excluded or priced separately.

02. SUBURB COVERAGE

Is service area coverage clear and consistent?

We compare the suburbs or regions stated on your website homepage, service pages, and footer against the service areas listed on your Google Business Profile.

03. ENQUIRY STEPS

Are quote-request details and next steps clear?

We inspect your contact pages and quote buttons to check whether customers are prompted to share the practical details you need to assess and answer an enquiry.

Who this service suits

Local Lift is designed for established, owner-managed residential cleaning businesses in Perth that already have a live website and Google Business Profile, and have either direct editing access or a web contact who publishes updates.

  • Multiple touchpoints to align: Your services, booking rules, or preferred suburbs have shifted over time, and you want your website pages, Google profile, and enquiry prompts checked together.
  • Ready-to-use copy without a full rebuild: You want specific replacement wording and placement notes rather than paying for a new website or an ongoing agency retainer.
  • Clear division of responsibility: You confirm your services, prices, policies, and promises; we draft the wording and map whether each item is published by you or your web contact.

When you may not need Local Lift

Not every website update requires an external review. Depending on what you need, a direct update or another service may be more suitable:

  • A simple known edit (DIY or existing web provider): If you already know the exact sentence or suburb list you want changed on one page, updating it yourself or sending a quick instruction to your web developer is usually faster.
  • Checking service areas yourself: You can use our free Coverage checklist independently to align your Google Business Profile and website without enquiring.
  • Broader marketing or technical rebuilds: If you need a brand-new website, technical search engine optimisation, paid ads, or full page-building implementation, a web development agency, SEO specialist, or full-service website copywriting Perth provider may fit better.
